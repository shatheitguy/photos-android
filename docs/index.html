Features

Everything you love about Photos.

Designed to feel instantly familiar, built natively for Android.

One library, beautifully organised

Every photo and video in a single grid, newest at the bottom.

  • Years, Months, Days and All Photos
  • Filter Favorites, Photos, Videos, Screenshots
  • Pinch to change grid size
  • Long-press and drag to select — auto-scrolls

Memories

Your best moments turned into slideshows, automatically.

  • On This Day
  • Weekends and busy days
  • Best of the month, Year in Review
  • Featured Photos every day

Edit without worry

Edits are saved as a copy. Your original is never changed.

  • Brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth
  • 10 filters — Vivid, Dramatic, Mono, Noir…
  • Rotate, press and hold to compare

A viewer that feels right

Swipe, zoom, scrub — every gesture you expect.

  • Scrub the thumbnail strip to fly through photos
  • Pinch and double-tap to zoom
  • Swipe down to close, up for details
  • Videos and GIFs play inline

Albums and utilities

Folders, favorites and smart media types in one place.

  • Videos, Selfies, Screenshots, Panoramas
  • Duplicates finder
  • Hidden and Recently Deleted, locked with your PIN

Search

Find anything by month, year, weekday, album or type.

  • Try “November”, “2025”, “videos”
  • Moments grouped by month

Privacy

Your photos stay on your phone.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

1

Download

Get Photos.apk from the latest release.

2

Install

Open the file on your phone. If Android asks, allow installing from this source.

3

Allow access

Open Photos and tap Continue so it can show your photos and videos.

4

Make it default

Open any photo from another app, choose Photos and tap Always.
